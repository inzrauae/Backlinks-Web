About this item

YouTube videos rank in Google's regular search results and in YouTube's own search algorithm, and both respond to backlinks the same way a normal webpage does — a signal most creators and video marketers never build deliberately. 500 YT SEO Backlinks closes that gap with video-embedded backlinks pointing directly at your YouTube video URL.

Each of the 500 placements embeds your video (or links directly to it) from relevant blog posts, Web 2.0 properties, and content pages, with surrounding text built around your target keywords so the link carries topical relevance in addition to raw authority. This dual signal — an embed plus a keyword-relevant surrounding page — is what search engines look for when deciding whether a video deserves a spot on page one.

This listing works well for any video that already has solid on-platform engagement (watch time, retention, comments) but has not broken through to page-one rankings, since it is specifically the off-platform authority signal, not the content itself, that is usually the missing piece for videos stuck in that position.

This listing works especially well for videos that already have solid retention and engagement metrics on-platform but have not broken through in search rankings, since it is specifically the off-platform authority and embed signal most creators never build deliberately. It is a natural complement to our larger YouTube Video SEO Ranking package for buyers who want to scale up the same strategy with added article support.

After you provide your video URL and target keywords, delivery begins and you will receive your live-URL report by email within 1 business day. Every placement is covered by our standard 12-month replacement guarantee.
