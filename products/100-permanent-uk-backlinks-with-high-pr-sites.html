If your business serves UK customers, ranking in google.co.uk depends on more than generic global links — it depends on relevance signals from sites Google already associates with the United Kingdom. This listing delivers 100 permanent backlinks sourced exclusively from UK-hosted and UK-registered domains with meaningful PageRank and organic visibility.
