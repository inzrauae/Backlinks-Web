About this item

When you just need a fast, affordable trust signal pointing at a new page, video, or profile, 100 Dofollow Backlinks For Any URL is built for exactly that job. It is the entry point into off-page SEO for site owners, affiliate marketers, and agencies who want to seed a link profile before investing in higher-ticket placements, without waiting weeks for outreach to land.

Each order builds out 100 dofollow links across a mixed pool of blog comments, forum profiles, Web 2.0 properties, and social bookmarking platforms. Anchor text is varied automatically between your target keyword, your brand name, and generic phrases like "click here" or "read more" so the resulting profile looks organic rather than manufactured — a detail that matters more to Google than raw link count ever will.

Because this tier is priced for volume, we are upfront about what it is and is not: it will not out-rank a competitor with genuine editorial coverage on its own, but it reliably nudges crawl frequency and indexing speed for new URLs, and it is a safe, low-risk way to round out a broader link-building campaign that also includes higher-authority placements from elsewhere in the INZRA catalog.

It works well for practically any URL type — a blog post, a landing page, an affiliate link, a social profile, or a freshly launched product page — which is why it is one of our most frequently reordered listings among buyers managing several small sites at once. If you are weighing this against our higher-authority DA 70+ or DA 90+ packages, think of this one as breadth rather than depth: it is not meant to replace those listings, it is meant to sit underneath them.

You will receive a delivery report by email within 1 business day listing every live URL so you can verify placement yourself. As with every listing on INZRA, if a link is removed or turns nofollow within 12 months of delivery, we replace it or refund that portion of your order — no questions asked.
