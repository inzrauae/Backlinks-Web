Homepage vs Inner-Page PBN Links: Which Passes More Authority?

21 July 2026 · 2 min read

Not every link on a DA 60 domain carries equal weight. A domain's accumulated authority isn't distributed evenly across its pages — the homepage typically holds the largest share of a site's internal link equity, since most inbound links and internal navigation point there first.

Why homepage position changes the math

A link placed directly on a source domain's homepage inherits a meaningfully larger share of that domain's authority than the identical link buried three clicks deep on an inner article page. A homepage PBN package is built specifically around this position advantage, which is part of why it commands a higher price than inner-page PBN links at a similar domain authority.

The trade-off: fewer available slots

Every domain has exactly one homepage, which means available homepage real estate across a network is inherently limited. Placements have to be rotated carefully to avoid concentrating too many customers' links on the same handful of pages — a constraint that doesn't exist for inner-page links, where a single domain can host dozens of article placements.

When to reach for homepage placements specifically

Use homepage PBN links for your single highest-priority page, not spread thin across many URLs — the speed and authority advantage is most valuable when concentrated. For everything else, inner-page contextual placements and a broader white-hat contextual campaign deliver more total coverage per dollar.

A mistake to avoid: spreading homepage links too thin

Because homepage slots are inherently limited across any PBN network, ordering this package for many different target URLs at once dilutes its main advantage. Concentrate homepage placements on your single highest-priority page rather than distributing them across a whole site.

What kind of timeline to expect

Given the position value and careful rotation required, expect delivery to unfold over several days rather than all at once. Ranking impact from homepage-level PBN links tends to be faster and more noticeable than inner-page placements at a similar DA, often visible within two to four weeks.

Frequently asked: how is homepage PBN pricing justified versus inner-page links?

Homepage placements are priced higher because the position itself is scarcer and more valuable — every domain has exactly one homepage versus potentially dozens of inner article pages, and that single slot carries a disproportionate share of the domain's total accumulated link equity. The premium reflects genuine scarcity and authority concentration, not just a marketing markup on an otherwise identical link type.

The bottom line: position on the source domain is a real, measurable factor in how much authority a link actually passes — not just a marketing distinction used to justify a higher price. Reserve homepage placements for the single page where that extra authority concentration will matter most, and use inner-page or contextual links for broader coverage elsewhere.
