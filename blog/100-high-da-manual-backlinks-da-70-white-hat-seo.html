How to Get DA 70+ Backlinks Without Getting Penalized

18 August 2026 · 3 min read

Domain Authority isn't a Google metric — it's Moz's proxy for how much ranking power a site is likely to pass. But proxy or not, buyers chase it for a reason: a link from a genuinely high-DA site correlates strongly with faster, larger ranking movement than the same link from a low-authority page. The catch is that most "DA 70+" offers on the open market are either wildly inflated metrics or links built in ways that put your own site at risk.

What actually makes a high-DA link safe

Three things separate a defensible DA 70+ placement from a liability: the source domain has real, verifiable organic traffic (not just a Moz score); the link sits inside genuinely relevant content rather than a sidebar widget; and the anchor text is proportioned naturally rather than exact-match. A manually built DA 70+ package that checks all three is doing something meaningfully different from an automated link blast with an inflated authority score attached.

Why manual placement changes the risk profile

Automated link networks get penalized in waves because Google can pattern-match them — same template, same anchor distribution, same velocity. Manual placement breaks that pattern by design: every link is reviewed individually, placed on a genuinely different type of site, and delivered on a staggered timeline rather than all at once. That's slower and more expensive to produce, which is exactly why it's rare at this price point.

Where DA 70+ links fit in a broader campaign

Use these as cornerstone links for your two or three highest-priority pages, not spread thin across an entire site. A handful of genuinely strong links on your money pages, backed by a wider volume layer like broader dofollow coverage elsewhere, mirrors how authority actually accumulates on sites that never bought a single link.

Common mistakes when buying "high DA" links

The biggest one is trusting a DA number without checking whether the site behind it has real organic traffic — plenty of domains game their Moz score with reciprocal or low-quality links while carrying almost no genuine visitors. A defensible high-DA package always lets you verify the placements yourself with a live-URL report, not just a promised metric.

What to expect in terms of timing and movement

Because placements are manually reviewed and staggered rather than dumped all at once, expect delivery to unfold over several days rather than instantly. Ranking movement from a small number of genuinely high-authority links tends to be more noticeable, and often faster, than the same budget spent on a much larger volume package — this is the tier where quality visibly outperforms quantity.

Frequently asked: how is DA 70+ different from DR (Domain Rating)?

Domain Authority is Moz's metric; Domain Rating is Ahrefs' equivalent, and the two don't always agree on a given site, since they're calculated from different link graphs and weighting formulas. A domain verified independently across both metrics is a stronger signal than one that only looks impressive in a single tool, which is why a genuinely high-quality DA 70+ source is usually also strong on DR, organic traffic estimates, and other independent measures — not just the one metric being advertised.

The bottom line: at this authority tier, quality and process matter more than almost anywhere else in link building, since a single flagged placement can undo the value of dozens of good ones. Verify sourcing, insist on real traffic data rather than an inflated score, and reserve these placements for the pages where authority genuinely moves the needle.
